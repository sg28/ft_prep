**8.Question**
**Why is it easier to manage narcissistic individuals**
**compared to Machiavellians or psychopaths?**
Answer:Narcissists are typically more visible in their
behavior, making them easier to identify. They respond well
to flattery and acknowledgment, which are tactics that many
can employ effectively. In contrast, Machiavellians and
psychopaths may be more deceptive, making them harder to
manage.

**4.Question**
**How does an 'as if' personality differ from a psychopathic**
**impostor?**
Answer:An 'as if' personality experiences their roles as
merely performative, lacking authentic belief in their social
identities. Unlike psychopathic impostors, they do not exhibit
cold or antisocial tendencies; they might feel more genuine
when pretending, as noted by entertainers like Tony
Blackburn.
**5.Question**
**What are some common characteristics shared by all**
**types of impostors?**
Answer:Impostors often lack a defined identity, are skilled
mimics, show verbal facility, and display emotional
attunement while often being oblivious to others' needs. They
may feel more alive when pretending and bored or lackluster
when engaging in their real roles.
