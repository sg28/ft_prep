**Chapter 12 | Dirty Tricks| Q&A**
**1.Question**
**What role do dirty tactics play in organizational success?**
Answer:Dirty tactics can significantly impact
success in an organization, either positively or
negatively. Used ethically, they can benefit both the
individual and the organization. However, they can
backfire or lead to severe consequences if executed
poorly. It's crucial to understand the motivations of
others and recognize when dirty tactics may be
employed.
**2.Question**
**How does blackmail function in workplace dynamics?**
Answer:Blackmail is a coercive tactic that involves
threatening someone to force a desired action. In the case of
Friedrich from the EU parliament, he leveraged sensitive
information about others to secure his career advancements,

**3.Question**
**Are there ethical scenarios where blackmail may be**
**justified?**
Answer:Yes, there are circumstances where using blackmail
could be seen as serving a greater ethical good, such as
protecting public interests. For example, Sofia anticipates

**4.Question**
**What is defamation and how can it be used strategically?**
Answer:Defamation is an impression management tactic that
harms someone's reputation, typically through subtle
influence. By planting doubts or framing negative questions
about a peer, one can manipulate perceptions without
creating a direct conflict or backlash, as illustrated by Sofia’s
missions to gather harmful information on rivals.

**5.Question**
**How do people typically engage in deception at work?**
Answer:Deception at work often includes telling white lies or
altering truths to avoid conflict or create a favorable
impression. This can occur frequently, as people lie about
one-fifth of their daily exchanges, often feeling there’s no
viable truthful alternative.
**6.Question**
**What are the risks associated with lying in the**
**workplace?**
Answer:Lying carries significant risks, including loss of
credibility, damaged relationships, and potential career
jeopardy if the truth comes out. Incidents of lying can also
lead to severe damage to personal or professional reputations,
as discussed with the example of Tim's machinations to
promote a colleague.
**7.Question**
**How does leadership influence the level of deceit in an**
**organization?**
Answer:Leaders set the tone for organizational culture.

<!-- Page 125 -->

Altruistic leaders can foster an environment where deceit is
tolerated if it serves a perceived greater good. Conversely,
self-interested leaders tend to breed distrust and deceit,
resulting in lower employee morale and engagement.

Employees are less committed and more dissatisfied in
environments perceived as dishonest.
**8.Question**
**What broad societal factors contribute to workplace**
**deceit?**
Answer:Factors such as insecure employment conditions,
outsourcing, low wages, and short contracts lead to
environments with higher deceit levels. In contrast,
organizations in stable economies tend to display more trust
and commitment among employees.
