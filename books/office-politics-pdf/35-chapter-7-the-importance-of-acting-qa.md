**Chapter 7 | The Importance of Acting| Q&A**
**1.Question**
**What is the role of acting in office politics according to**
**the chapter?**
Answer:Acting is essential for navigating office
politics, as it influences how others perceive you and
can help you achieve specific goals. By consciously
adopting a persona when necessary, you can
manipulate perceptions to your advantage without
feeling guilt or self-consciousness.
**2.Question**
**How can one use acting to influence a boss's perception?**
Answer:To influence your boss's perception, present yourself
in a way that aligns with their expectations. This might
include mirroring their language or demeanor, and subtly
demonstrating your skills in relation to their needs.

**5.Question**

with lower-level colleagues for support while managing

political, sowing doubt about competitors while maintaining
a facade of collegiality.

**6.Question**
**What warning does Charlie's story provide about**
**extreme forms of acting in professional environments?**
Answer:Charlie's case warns against the sustainability of
extreme acting. Constantly adopting a false persona can lead
to emotional exhaustion and an inability to form genuine
relationships, ultimately jeopardizing personal well-being.
**7.Question**
**According to the chapter, what differentiates**
**achievement-oriented individuals from those motivated**
**intrinsically?**
Answer:Achievement-oriented individuals are primarily
driven by mastery and status, often employing political skills
for success. In contrast, intrinsically motivated individuals
seek personal satisfaction and emotional growth, which can
also lead to superior performance when combined with
political skills.

**How can one cultivate better political skills in the**
**workplace?**
Answer:You can enhance your political skills by being
observant and adaptable, learning to read situations and
