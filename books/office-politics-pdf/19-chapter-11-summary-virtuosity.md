**Chapter 11 Summary : Virtuosity**
**Chapter 11: Virtuosity**
**Overview**
Success in a career requires more than being dutiful, talented,
and nice; perception of virtue is crucial. Traits like
conscientiousness, honesty, and rationality can be
advantageous when effectively managed as part of
impression management.
**Conscientiousness**
Hard work often leads to higher earnings and positions, yet
political skills are equally important. Over-conscientiousness
can harm physical and mental health, making it essential to
balance personal interests with professional duties. A
middle-ranking manager exemplifies the need for
self-promotion through the strategic communication of hard
work, emphasizing visibility over mere diligence.

Recognizing the importance of being seen as collaborative

<!-- Page 53 -->

and engaged in the workplace helps avoid the negative label
of selfishness, which can hinder long-term career prospects.
**Honesty**
Navigating the tension between authenticity and professional
demands is challenging. While aiming for honesty can be a
good strategy, many individuals engage in forms of
deception without realizing it. Giuseppe, a pharmaceuticals
executive, views himself as honest, despite acting disloyally.

His strategy of being upfront with his boss seems to
strengthen his credibility, though he often fails to
acknowledge his own omissions. Balancing honesty with
political savvy is vital; overly ingratiating tactics can lead to
damaging perceptions if discovered. Being true to oneself,
while adapting when necessary, generally supports career
success.
**Rationality**
A well-argued rational case is important, but personal
dynamics often overshadow rational arguments in
decision-making. External factors like office politics,
personal grievances, and random events can skew outcomes.

Thus, one must remain strategically aware of the limits of
rationality in influence. Recognizing when to withhold
rational views, as demonstrated by a health service
administrator in a meeting, can be more beneficial than
pursuing a losing argument. Successful professionals must
integrate conscientiousness, honesty, and rationality with
adept political skill to navigate their work environments
effectively.
