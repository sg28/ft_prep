**Chapter 11 | Virtuosity| Q&A**
**1.Question**
**What is the essential balance one must strike in office**
**settings to be successful?**
Answer:One must balance virtue with political
savvy, meaning it's essential to be seen as
conscientious, honest, and rational, but also to be
aware of the importance of impression management
and political skills in ensuring one's efforts are
recognized.
**2.Question**
**Why might hard work alone not lead to success in a**
**workplace?**
Answer:Hard work alone may not lead to success because it
must be paired with political awareness and the ability to
communicate one’s contributions effectively to
decision-makers. Without political skills, even the most
diligent worker might struggle to get acknowledged.
**3.Question**
**How can perception of virtue generate business success?**

Answer:Perception of virtue, such as being conscientious,
can generate business success by creating trust and
credibility among peers and superiors, leading to greater
opportunities and favoritism in promotions, as long as one
effectively manages how they are seen by others.
**4.Question**
**What role does honesty play in office politics according to**
**Oliver James?**
Answer:Honesty can function as a beneficial strategy in
office politics when presented authentically; however, it
should be balanced with the understanding that some degree
of strategic communication and omission may be necessary
to navigate complex workplace dynamics.

**6.Question**
**What happens if one ignores the importance of office**
**politics while trying to remain virtuous?**
Answer:Ignoring the importance of office politics while
trying to remain virtuous can result in career stagnation, as
one may fail to advocate for themselves effectively, leading
to a lack of recognition and rewards despite potentially high
merit.
**7.Question**
**In what way can working hard during specific times be**
**strategically beneficial?**
Answer:Working hard during key periods, like from January
to April when bosses are more likely to remember your
contributions at appraisal time, can create a lasting
impression that positively influences future evaluations, even
if one eases off at other times.
