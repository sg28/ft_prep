**Chapter 9 | Ingratiation| Q&A**
**1.Question**
**What is the fundamental principle behind the**
**effectiveness of ingratiation in the workplace?**
Answer:Ingratiation is effective because when you
need something from someone, if you can make
them like you by making them feel good, they are
more likely to give you what you want.
**2.Question**
**How can understanding chameleonism help in workplace**
**dynamics?**

Answer:Understanding chameleonism can help you adapt
your behavior to fit in with others, making them feel more
comfortable, which in turn can facilitate better working
relationships and open up opportunities for advancement.
**3.Question**
**What are the three main techniques of ingratiation**
**discussed in the chapter?**
Answer:The three main techniques are chameleonism,
flattery, and favour-rendering.
**4.Question**
**How does chameleonism manifest in workplace behavior?**
Answer:Chameleonism manifests as mirroring another
person's mannerisms or speech patterns, allowing an
individual to create a sense of similarity and connection,
which can lead to improved interpersonal relations and the
potential to gain favors.
**5.Question**
**In what ways can flattery be a double-edged sword in**
**professional settings?**

**6.Question**
**Can you provide an example of how favour-rendering can**
**lead to professional advancement?**
Answer:Favour-rendering can lead to advancement, as
demonstrated by Sofia who, by helping Miguel with tasks he
disliked, ultimately gained his trust and became
indispensable in her role.
**7.Question**
**What should individuals consider when engaging in**
**ingratiation to ensure it is beneficial?**
Answer:Individuals should ensure that their ingratiation
efforts are sincere and not overdone; excessive ingratiation
may come off as disingenuous, while moderate use can
enhance relationships and career prospects.

**What impact might constant chameleons have on an**

**9.Question**
**Why might non-chameleons face career progression**
**challenges compared to constant chameleons?**
Answer:Non-chameleons might face challenges in career
progression because their commitment to authenticity may be
perceived as less flexible or adaptable, making it harder to fit
into the dynamic office culture that often rewards
chameleon-like behavior.
**10.Question**
**What lesson can be drawn about the balance between**
**being a chameleon and maintaining authenticity at work?**
Answer:The lesson is that while some degree of
chameleonism is often necessary to fit in and succeed
professionally, it should be exercised sparingly to maintain
personal satisfaction and authenticity.
