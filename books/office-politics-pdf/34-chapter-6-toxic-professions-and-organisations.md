**Q&A**
**1.Question**
**What are the implications of increasing triadic behavior**

<!-- Page 95 -->

**in workplaces?**
Answer:The increasing prevalence of triadic
behavior—psychopathy, narcissism, and
Machiavellianism—in the workplace leads to toxic
organizational cultures. This can result in
diminished job satisfaction, increased stress among
employees, and a focus on manipulation as a key
strategy for success, rather than merit-based
achievements. As organizations become more
politically charged, individuals who possess these
traits may rise to power while those who do not may
struggle to be recognized or rewarded.
**2.Question**
**How can individuals navigate toxic workplaces**
**characterized by triadic behavior?**
Answer:To navigate toxic workplace environments,
individuals must develop strong office political skills. This
includes understanding the dynamics of power, being
self-aware, and employing strategic communication.

<!-- Page 96 -->

Building alliances, being adaptable, and maintaining
professionalism are crucial strategies that can help in
managing relationships and protecting oneself from toxic
influence.
**3.Question**
**What does the case of Natasha at the law firm illustrate**
**about workplace dynamics?**
Answer:Natasha's experience highlights the brutal reality of
meritocracies that only reward relentless commitment and
exceptional skill. It underscores how toxic environments can
exploit workers, particularly women, who seek recognition
but receive minimal support in a system geared towards
competition and ruthless evaluation. The need to balance
ambition with emotional resilience is critical in such settings.
**4.Question**
**Why is public sector work increasingly susceptible to**
**toxic triadic behavior?**
Answer:Public sector work is becoming more susceptible to
triadic behavior due to increased bureaucracy and

<!-- Page 97 -->

performance measurement pressures, leading to a 'watch your
back' mentality. Outsourcing services creates ambiguity in
accountability, enabling toxic individuals to maneuver their
way into power, fostering an environment where
manipulation is more prevalent than collaboration.
**5.Question**
**What distinguishes journalism from television production**
**in terms of workplace culture and accountability?**
Answer:Journalism is typically characterized by clearer
accountability, where individuals' contributions are more
easily identified through bylines and editorial standards. In
contrast, television production fosters an environment where
political maneuvering and personal connections often
overshadow merit, leading to a culture where charming but
less skilled individuals may thrive and dictate narratives.
**6.Question**
**What role does individualism versus collectivism play in**
**workplace dynamics?**
Answer:Individualistic societies often reward self-promotion

<!-- Page 98 -->

and assertiveness, facilitating the rise of triadic behaviors. In
contrast, collectivist cultures emphasize group harmony and
shared responsibility, which can restrain the visibility of
toxic traits, leading to healthier workplace dynamics where
cooperation is valued over ruthless ambition.
**7.Question**
**How can an understanding of triadic traits inform hiring**
**practices in organizations?**
Answer:Organizations that acknowledge the prevalence of
triadic traits can refine their hiring practices to focus less on
personality fit and more on competencies, accountability, and
collaborative potential. By developing structured interview
processes that assess both technical skills and interpersonal
dynamics, workplaces can cultivate healthier environments.
**8.Question**
**What strategies can organizations adopt to mitigate the**
**rise of triadic individuals?**

transparent performance reviews, collective goal-setting, and

intelligence and conflict resolution can also help in reducing
toxic influences and promoting a more positive workplace
