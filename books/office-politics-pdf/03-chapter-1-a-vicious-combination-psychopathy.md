**Chapter 1: A Vicious Combination: Psychopathy,**
**Machiavellianism, and Narcissism**
In this chapter, the author addresses the challenges posed by
toxic individuals in the workplace, metaphorically referred to
as "predators." These people, often characterized by traits
such as psychopathy, Machiavellianism, and narcissism
collectively known as the "Dark Triad," can create significant
distress for their colleagues. Recognizing and understanding
these traits is crucial for navigating office politics and
maintaining one's well-being.
**Identifying Toxic Individuals**
The text introduces a test to help readers identify problematic
colleagues by scoring them on 12 specific traits related to the
Dark Triad. Individuals who score above 25 points are likely
to display disagreeableness and aggressive behavior.

Common traits of those exhibiting these personality
characteristics include:
- Exploitation and manipulation
- Lack of empathy and remorse 
- Desire for admiration and dominance
