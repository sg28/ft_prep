**Chapter 3: Machiavels**
**Traits of Machiavels**
- Resistant to social influences and hides personal
convictions.
- Changes arguments easily and is convincing in the truth.
- Resistant to admitting mistakes and suspicious of others'
motives.
- Expects no reciprocity in relationships and withholds
judgment on others’ motives.
- Adaptable strategies and exploitative, but not excessively
vicious.
- Not influenced by appeals for compliance and prefers
dynamic environments.
- Often preferred as leaders and work partners by peers.
**Traits of Non-Machiavels**
- Vulnerable to social influences and displays convictions
openly.
- Sticks rigidly to beliefs and readily confesses mistakes.
