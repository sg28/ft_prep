**Chapter 10 | Go-getting| Q&A**
**1.Question**
**What are the key tactics discussed in Chapter 10 for**
**advancing in the workplace?**
Answer:The key tactics for advancing in the
workplace include assertiveness, self-promotion,
feedback-seeking, negotiations, networking, and
reputation-building.
**2.Question**
**How can assertiveness differ from aggression in the**
**workplace?**
Answer:Assertiveness involves calmly insisting on your
views and setting boundaries without expressing anger or
hostility, while aggression implies hostility and the intent to
harm or dominate others. Assertive individuals communicate
clearly about their needs and opinions while remaining open
to others' perspectives.
**3.Question**
**Why is self-promotion a tricky endeavor?**
Answer:Self-promotion can be risky because it runs the

<!-- Page 115 -->

danger of being perceived as boastful, inciting envy or
competitiveness among colleagues. However, when done
skillfully, it can lead to appreciation and recognition of one's
talents and achievements.
**4.Question**
**What are some reasons people avoid seeking feedback in**
**the workplace?**
Answer:People might avoid seeking feedback due to fear of
negative evaluation, potential harm to their pride, or concerns
about how it may affect their reputation among peers. Many
prefer only positive feedback as it bolsters self-esteem.
**5.Question**
**What does the chapter suggest about cultural differences**
**in seeking feedback?**
Answer:The chapter highlights that in individualistic cultures
like the U.S., people tend to seek feedback on personal
achievements, while in collectivist cultures, like Japan and
China, individuals may prioritize group performance
feedback to avoid drawing individual attention.

**6.Question**
**How important is networking according to Chapter 10,**
**and what does it entail?**
Answer:Networking is crucial for career advancement; it
involves building relationships with individuals who may not
have immediate utility but can provide support in the future.

This includes sharing information and establishing coalitions
that can provide social capital later on.
**7.Question**
**What role does reputation play in professional growth**
**according to the chapter?**
Answer:Reputation significantly influences promotion
prospects, job offers, and earning potential. A favorable
reputation leads to increased trust, status, and power within
an organization, directly affecting one's career trajectory.
**8.Question**
**How does the chapter advise individuals to approach pay**
**negotiations?**
Answer:The chapter advises starting discussions on pay
midway through the year rather than during formal annual

reviews. It’s vital to prepare in advance by establishing a
good relationship with your boss and communicating your
contributions, setting the stage for successful negotiations.
**9.Question**
**Can you give an example of effective assertiveness from**
**the content?**
Answer:An example of effective assertiveness from the
chapter is Sofia, an intern who firmly insisted she did not
want to be associated with a questionable expenses claim
from her boss. She confidently communicated her stance,
protecting her integrity and future career.
**10.Question**
**According to the chapter, what makes someone effective**
**in building a good reputation?**
Answer:Factors contributing to a good reputation include
having strong educational credentials, effective networking
skills, and a positive style characterized by affability,
self-control, and proactivity. These traits enhance one’s
political effectiveness and career growth.
