**Chapter 6: Toxic Professions and Organisations**
**Overview of Triadic Behaviour**
Over the past thirty years, the prevalence of psychopathy,
narcissism, and Machiavellianism has significantly increased,
particularly in America, with similar trends noted in other
English-speaking countries. The chapter discusses how these
traits manifest in various professions, suggesting that toxic
behaviour is increasingly widespread in workplaces.
**Experiences in Various Professions**
The author, Oliver James, shares different career experiences,
including his time in television production, academia, and
journalism, emphasizing the varying levels of triadic toxicity
encountered.

**Television Production Case Study**
James highlights the toxic dynamics in a particular television
project, "A Poke With Pat," revealing how the characters
involved demonstrated traits of psychopathy and narcissism.

The project was marked by office politics, undermining
behaviours, and dysfunctional relationships, significantly
affecting the quality of the final product.
**Toxic Traits in Television Professionals**
Through an analysis of individuals involved in the
production, James illustrates how many in the television
industry exhibit triadic traits, often resulting in a lack of
empathy and a focus on self-interest over collective goals.

This toxic culture leads to cutthroat politics where
manipulation and ruthlessness predominate.
