# STAR Story 6 — Conflict / Disagreement (DRAFT TEMPLATE)

> ⚠️ **This story is invented, not a real account of your work.** Same caveat as [Story 5](story-5-failure.md): the plan marks this "TO DRAFT" with no underlying content to draw from. This is a template shape only — **replace it with a real disagreement before using it in an interview.**

## Criteria (from the plan)

- Disagreed with a senior person or peer
- Navigated it professionally
- Good outcome — even if the outcome wasn't "you got your way"

## Template Shape (pick the pattern closest to something real from your career)

**Pattern:** Pushed back on a technical decision with data, got the call reversed or reached a compromise.

> **Set up the disagreement clearly:** [What was the decision being made, what was your position, what was the other person's position, and what was actually at stake if the wrong call was made — e.g. a scalability risk, a maintenance burden, a timeline risk.]
>
> **Show you brought data, not just opinions:** [What specifically did you bring — a benchmark, a spike/prototype, a cost estimate, a comparison with a prior incident? "I disagreed but couldn't back it with anything concrete" is a much weaker answer than a specific artifact.]
>
> **Show you stayed professional even when you didn't get your way (or even if you did):** [How was the disagreement actually resolved — a meeting, a written proposal, an escalation? Name the mechanism, not just the outcome.]
>
> **End with the outcome:** [If you "won" — show humility, e.g. "the other person was reasonable and their initial position wasn't unfounded, they just hadn't seen the data yet." If you "lost" — show you committed fully afterward rather than quietly resenting the decision or dragging on execution.]

## Patterns to Avoid (from the plan)

- Framing the other person as wrong or foolish
- "I was right and they were wrong" without nuance
- Stories with no real conflict ("we politely discussed and aligned") — this reads as if you're avoiding the question, not as evidence of maturity

## Follow-Ups to Prep For (once you have a real story here)

- What was the other person's strongest argument, and did it have merit?
- If you'd lost this one, what would you have done?
- Has your relationship with that person changed since?
