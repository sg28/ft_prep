# Recruiter Screen Answer — What They'll Ask

The first call sets your level, comp band, and what gets relayed to the panel — these answers matter more than they seem.

## "Walk me through your background"

Use the 90-second pitch — see [../behavioral/question-story-map.md](../behavioral/question-story-map.md).

## "What are you looking for?"

> "A Lead role with end-to-end ownership, ideally in fintech or applied AI."

Don't say "anything I can get" — it signals you don't actually have a direction, which makes it harder for the recruiter to advocate for the right level/team internally.

## "What's your current comp?"

> "I'd rather discuss the range for this role first."

If pressed and you have to answer: give **total comp** (base + bonus + equity), not just base — quoting base alone can anchor the recruiter to a lower number than your actual current compensation.

## "What's your target?"

> "I'm interviewing at several companies in the $X–$Y total comp range, but the role fit matters more to me than maxing a number."

This gives a real range without naming a hard floor that could become a ceiling — see the full negotiation scripts in the plan's §10 for how this plays out later in the process.

## "Why are you looking?"

> "Looking to own more end-to-end systems, and lean into applied AI in production rather than prototypes."

Positive framing only — never say anything negative about a current or former employer, manager, or team, no matter how justified it might feel. Recruiters read this as a signal about how you'll talk about *them* someday.
