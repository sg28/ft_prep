# Recruiter Screen — Questions to Ask Them

These aren't questions to *answer* — they're what you ask the recruiter during the screen. Each one is listed with why it's worth asking.

| Question | Why it's worth asking |
|---|---|
| "What level is this role posted at internally?" | Gets you concrete clarity on "Principal Associate" vs "Senior Associate" — internal titles can be ambiguous from the outside, and this affects both scope and comp band. |
| "What's the comp band for this level in this region?" | Gives you a real anchor before any negotiation conversation happens — see the plan's §10 negotiation scripts, which assume you already know the band. |
| "Who will be on Power Day and what does each round focus on?" | Lets you tailor prep — knowing whether the system design round leans toward a specific domain (payments vs fraud vs general) changes what to rehearse hardest. |
| "What's the typical timeline from Power Day to offer?" | Sets expectations so you're not left guessing whether silence means rejection — also useful for coordinating competing offers/timelines. |
| "Is this a backfill or new headcount?" | A new headcount role usually has more scope flexibility; a backfill role is more likely to be shaped tightly around what the previous person did. |
