# Interviewer Questions — What to Ask Them

Not questions to answer — these are what *you* ask at the end of each round. End every round with 1-2 of them, and don't repeat the same one across rounds.

## For Engineers

| Question | Why it's worth asking |
|---|---|
| "Path from prototype to production for AI/ML projects here?" | Tests whether the company's AI story is real production work or perpetual pilot projects — directly relevant if you're pitching applied-AI experience. |
| "Where does engineering autonomy stop and platform/governance begin?" | Surfaces how much you'd actually own vs how much is dictated by a central platform team — important at the Lead level where scope of ownership matters. |
| "Most surprising thing about working here?" | An open-ended question that tends to get a more honest, unscripted answer than a directly evaluative one. |
| "What's the on-call burden like for this team?" | A practical quality-of-life question that's easy to ask an engineer peer but might feel awkward to ask a hiring manager directly. |

## For the Hiring Manager

| Question | Why it's worth asking |
|---|---|
| "What does 'great' look like at 6 months? 12 months?" | Gets a concrete definition of success for the role — useful both for evaluating fit and for demonstrating you think in outcomes. |
| "Biggest unsolved technical problem on your team right now?" | Reveals real, current technical challenges rather than a polished summary of past wins — also a natural way to show genuine interest in the work itself. |
| "Team composition — senior/junior mix, locations, time zones?" | Practical info about team structure that affects day-to-day collaboration and mentorship expectations. |
| "What's the team's relationship with product and design?" | Surfaces how cross-functional the role actually is — relevant given the "cross-functional translation" strength story. |

## For the Recruiter

| Question | Why it's worth asking |
|---|---|
| "Offer + decision timeline?" | Practical logistics — helps you plan around competing processes. |
| "Typical comp range for this level?" | Same rationale as the recruiter-screen version — see [../recruiter-screen/questions-to-ask.md](../recruiter-screen/questions-to-ask.md); worth confirming again if it wasn't fully answered earlier. |
| "Power Day panel composition and focus areas?" | Same rationale as the recruiter-screen version — useful to re-confirm as Power Day approaches and the actual panel gets finalized. |
