# Sample Company Policy Document

## Employee Request Process

### Overview
All internal requests must be submitted through the company portal. This document outlines the standard procedures for various types of requests.

### Types of Requests

#### 1. Time Off Requests
- Submit at least 2 weeks in advance
- Manager approval required
- HR notification automatic
- Maximum 3 consecutive weeks per request

#### 2. Equipment Requests
- Submit through IT portal
- Justification required
- Budget approval for items over $500
- Standard delivery time: 5-7 business days

#### 3. Training Requests
- Must align with career development plan
- Manager approval required
- Budget code needed
- Complete within 6 months of approval

### Request Status Tracking
You can check request status by:
- Logging into the employee portal
- Checking email notifications
- Contacting your manager
- Reaching out to HR for urgent matters

### Contact Information
- HR Department: hr@company.com
- IT Support: itsupport@company.com
- Manager: Direct contact through Teams

### Approval Timeline
- Time off: 3-5 business days
- Equipment: 1-2 weeks
- Training: 2-3 weeks
- Budget-related: Up to 4 weeks

For questions about policies, please refer to the employee handbook or contact HR.
