# Deal Negotiation Strategy — Upwork Mastery Course ($1,250)

## Objective
Restructure the $1,250 coaching deal to maximize value, minimize risk, and align incentives between you and the coach.

---

## 1. Split the Payment into Milestones (Never Pay Upfront in Full)

| Milestone | Payment | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **Profile goes live** | $315 (25%) | After profile is optimized and published |
| **First 15 proposals sent** | $310 (25%) | After he reviews and you submit 15 proposals |
| **First client landed** | $315 (25%) | When you get your first paid contract |
| **3rd client landed** | $310 (25%) | When you close your 3rd client |

**Why this works:** It ties HIS income to YOUR results. If he's confident in his coaching, he'll accept. If he refuses, he's selling information — not outcomes.

---

## 2. Get a Written Agreement with These Clauses

Draft a simple 1-page agreement (or email confirmation) that includes:

### Deliverables he must provide:
- Fully optimized Upwork profile (title, overview, portfolio, skills) — written/reviewed by him
- Minimum 30 proposals reviewed by him before you submit
- Weekly 1-on-1 sessions (minimum 2 hours/week) for the duration
- Niche selection with market research backing
- 3 proposal templates customized for your niche
- A pricing strategy document specific to your skills
- Access to him via WhatsApp/Slack for quick questions between sessions

### Timeline commitment:
- Complete Module 1–3 within first 2 weeks (profile live and bidding)
- First client target: within 30 days
- 3 clients target: within 60–90 days

### Protection clause:
- If you haven't landed your first client within 45 days of starting proposals, he provides an additional 10 hours of coaching at no extra cost
- If you haven't landed ANY client within 90 days, 50% refund ($625)

---

## 3. Negotiate Add-Ons (Cost Him Nothing, Huge Value to You)

These are things that cost him zero extra effort but massively increase your ROI:

| Ask For | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| **Lifetime access to him for quick questions** (even after 35 hours) | You'll have questions 3–6 months in. A 5-minute WhatsApp reply costs him nothing |
| **His personal proposal templates** | Templates he uses for his own bidding — not generic ones |
| **Screen recording of him optimizing YOUR profile** | You can re-watch and learn his thought process |
| **Introduction to 2–3 of his existing clients** | If he's established, a warm referral is worth more than 35 hours of coaching |
| **One profile audit 3 months after the program ends** | Free follow-up to course-correct |

- Keeps HIM accountable (he can't just talk theory for 35 hours)
- Gives YOU data to know if his coaching is actually working

---

## 5. The "Break-Even" Pitch

Frame it like this when you negotiate:

> *"I'm ready to invest $1,250, but I want to structure it so we're both incentivized. If your coaching works — and I believe it will — I'll earn back the $1,250 within my first 2–3 clients. I'll also refer other people to you. But I need skin in the game from your side too. Can we tie payments to milestones?"*

This is powerful because:
- You're NOT saying "I don't trust you"
- You're saying "let's align incentives"
- You're offering him future referrals (which is real value to a coach)

---

## 6. The Nuclear Option (If He Pushes Back on Everything)

If he insists on full payment upfront, no milestones, no guarantees — counter with:

> *"Let's do a paid 3-hour trial session for $150. You optimize my profile in those 3 hours. If I'm impressed with the quality, I'll pay the remaining $1,100 for the full program."*

**$150 for 3 hours is $50/hour** — fair for him. And you get to see:
- Does he actually know Upwork deeply?
- Is his coaching style practical or just theory?
- Does your profile look noticeably better after his touch?

If he won't even do this, **walk away**.

---

## Summary: Your Ideal Deal Structure

| Element | What You Get |
|---|---|
| **Payment** | 5 milestone-based installments of $250 |
| **Guarantee** | Extra 10 hours free if no client in 45 days; 50% refund if no client in 90 days |
| **Deliverables** | Optimized profile, 30 reviewed proposals, 3 custom templates, pricing doc |
| **Ongoing access** | WhatsApp/Slack for quick questions even after program |
| **Follow-up** | Free profile audit at 3 months |
| **Accountability** | Weekly tracked metrics in shared spreadsheet |
| **Trial option** | 3-hour paid trial for $150 before full commitment |

---

## Key Principle

**Whoever has more to lose should carry more risk — right now that's you, so shift some of it to him.**

If he accepts even 60–70% of this, you've got a solid deal.
