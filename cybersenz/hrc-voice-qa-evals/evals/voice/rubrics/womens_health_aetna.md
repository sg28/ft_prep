# Scoring Rubric — Women's Health / Aetna
# MOMUS v0.1.0

Score the call across 5 dimensions. Dimension scores must sum to total_score.

---

## Data Completeness (0–30 points)

Did the agent capture all required insurance fields during the call?

Required fields:
- Member ID / subscriber ID
- Group number
- Deductible (individual and/or family)
- Out-of-pocket maximum (individual and/or family)
- Co-pay / co-insurance amounts for the relevant service
- Prior authorization requirement (yes/no — if yes, the contact or fax number)
- Effective date of coverage
- IVF / infertility treatment coverage (covered or not, and any limits)

Scoring guide:
- 28–30: All required fields captured and verbally confirmed
- 22–27: Most fields captured; 1–2 missing or left ambiguous
- 15–21: Several fields missing; call produced partial data
- 0–14: Major gaps; call was largely unproductive for data collection

---

## Conversation Quality (0–20 points)

Was the call professional, natural, and humanistic?

- Purpose stated clearly and concisely at the start
- Practice name and caller credentials provided naturally
- Responses were coherent, on-topic, and appropriately paced
- No unnecessary repetition, looping, or off-script tangents
- Language was professional and appropriate for a HIPAA medical billing call

Scoring guide:
- 18–20: Professional, natural, efficient — reads like a skilled human caller
- 13–17: Generally good with minor awkwardness
- 7–12: Noticeable issues with phrasing, repetition, or clarity
- 0–6: Significant quality problems that likely impacted rep cooperation

---

## Navigation Efficiency (0–20 points)

Did the agent navigate the IVR and reach the correct rep efficiently?

- IVR navigated without unnecessary loops or dead-ends
- Correct department reached on first or second attempt
- Human rep reached within a reasonable time
- No unrecoverable IVR failures or call drops during navigation

Scoring guide:
- 18–20: Clean navigation, minimal IVR time, correct department first attempt
- 13–17: Minor inefficiency (one extra menu level or brief confusion)
- 7–12: Noticeable navigation problems (multiple loops or wrong department initially)
- 0–6: IVR navigation failed or human rep was never reached

---

## Error Recovery (0–15 points)

How well did the agent handle unexpected situations?

- Handled rep confusion or requests for clarification gracefully
- Recovered from misheard input or incorrect information given by rep
- Managed hold periods without dropping or losing track of the call
- Adapted when rep gave conflicting or incomplete information

Scoring guide:
- 13–15: Excellent recovery from all obstacles encountered
- 9–12: Handled most issues well; one minor stumble
- 4–8: Some errors not recovered from; conversation stalled at points
- 0–3: Poor recovery; errors compounded and affected call outcome

---

## Call Closure (0–15 points)

Did the agent close the call correctly and completely?

- Reference or confirmation number captured (if provided by rep)
- Rep name noted
- All captured data verbally confirmed or summarised before ending
- Proper sign-off with appropriate professional closing phrase
- Call ended intentionally — no abrupt drop or silence

Scoring guide:
- 13–15: Complete and professional closure; reference number captured
- 9–12: Minor gap in closure (one item missed or not confirmed)
- 4–8: Incomplete closure; missing reference number or no data confirmation
- 0–3: Call dropped abruptly or no closure attempted
